Each step takes the lower of two limits: what the engine can push (torque × gearing, minus losses and rotating mass) and what the rear tyres can hold (grip × rear load, with weight transfer). Until the clutch is fully engaged, the engine stays at launch rpm and its inertia is not counted.

Power is shown in BHP as on the dyno sheet (Nm × rpm / 7023.5). Treat results as ±0.5 s. A GPS timing beats any model.
