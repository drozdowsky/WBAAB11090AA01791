# Roadmap

Ideas and planned work. When something is done, delete it here and add it to [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Engine
- [ ] Barrel / slide throttle bodies for better airflow (ETB?)
- [ ] Catcams
  - 1301414 (preferred, keeps low end): 268°/267° @ 0.1 mm, 234°/228° @ 1.0 mm, lift 11.50/10.95 mm
  - 1301415 (more top end, weaker below 4000 rpm): 279°/274° @ 0.1 mm, 243°/236° @ 1.0 mm, lift 11.50/10.95 mm
  - Solid (mechanical) buckets, to raise the rev limit to 8000 rpm
  - Light retainers + matching keepers for 7 mm stems
  - ARP rod bolts (needed for 8000 rpm)
- [ ] Check or replace the crank damper
- [ ] Intake charge pipe: carbon, tapered, with a straight silicone adapter
- [ ] Replace the stainless plenum wall with a carbon one (to match the carbon plenum)
- [ ] T-piece 18x16x18 for the intake plenum
- [ ] Bigger oil pan with baffles
- [ ] Dry sump
- [ ] Shorten the ignition loom
- [ ] Engine pulleys: new aluminium design, or repaint
- [ ] WOSP high-torque starter (lightweight)
